### Risk note on the passive-payment projects

Four of the five China-eligible listings ask the tester to hand over bank account, card, or wallet details and then receive incoming funds. Even hosted on a legitimate Applause platform, that shape matches money-mule recruitment, and one listing additionally asks for a *relative's* mainland China account. Under mainland regulation, lending or renting out account access for third-party funds carries real legal exposure. Do not enter bank details for any of these without first confirming through official uTest Tester Support that the specific cycle is client-sponsored, and never use a relative's account.
